TAARIIKHDA FED

Taabo sannad, arag bil kasta: FED-ku ma kor u qaaday, ma dhimay, mise wuu hayay?

Xogta dulsaarka, sicir-bararka, shaqo la'aanta iyo shaqooyinka laga bilaabo 1954 ilaa maanta. Waxay si toos ah uga imanaysaa FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis).

Xogta taariikhda waa la soo qaadayaa…
Kor u qaadis Dhimis Isbeddel ma jirin

Ka hor 1994, FED-ku go'aannadiisa si rasmi ah uma shaacin jirin. Ka hor 1982, xogta waxay muujinaysaa dulsaarka suuqa (effective rate), maxaa yeelay ma jiro diiwaan dulsaar hadaf ah (target).
